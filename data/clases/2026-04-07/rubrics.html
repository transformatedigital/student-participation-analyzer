⚙️ Grading Methodology

📐 Weighted Formula

Final Grade = (Responses × 50%) + (Questions × 30%) + (Comments × 20%) + Bonus

Example (Aryang): (2.80 × 0.50) + (3.10 × 0.30) + (2.88 × 0.20) + 0.15 = 3.12 (C)

Why these weights? Direct responses to instructor questions represent higher-order thinking and accuracy (50%). Student-initiated questions show critical thinking (30%). General comments show engagement but less rigor (20%).

🔍 Evaluation Process

Step 1: Transcription & Extraction

Audio is transcribed and all student statements are extracted with timestamps and categorized as responses, questions, or comments.

Step 2: Quality Assessment

Each statement is evaluated against the appropriate rubric (A for responses, B for questions/comments). Evaluators consider content, relevance, depth, and contribution to class discussion.

Step 3: Weighted Calculation

Grades for each type (responses, questions, comments) are averaged separately, then combined using the weighted formula with bonuses applied based on participation volume.

Step 4: Final Grade Assignment

Final numeric grade is converted to letter grade and assigned to student. Grades are capped at 5.0 (maximum) and 1.0 (minimum).
