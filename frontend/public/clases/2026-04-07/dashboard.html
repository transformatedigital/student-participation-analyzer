⚙️ Methodology Weighted Grading System How final grades are calculated with quality-based weighting

📊 Grading Formula

Each student's final grade combines quality scores from different participation types, with higher weight on direct responses to teacher questions:

Final Grade = (Weighted Average) + (Participation Bonus)

Weighted Average:
  (Responses Avg × 50%) + (Questions Avg × 30%) + (Comments Avg × 20%)

Participation Bonus:
  +0.15 if ≥40 participations
  +0.10 if ≥20 participations
  −0.20 if <5 participations
↩️ Responses (50%)

Direct answers to teacher questions. Most valuable for demonstrating understanding and engagement.

❓ Questions (30%)

Student-initiated questions. Shows critical thinking and deepens classroom discourse.

💬 Comments (20%)

Voluntary contributions and remarks. Counts toward participation but weighted lower than responses or questions.

⚠️ Important

Students with fewer than 5 participations receive a penalty (−0.20) to ensure grades are based on sufficient data. This system rewards quality and consistency while acknowledging high engagement.
